Studio FAQ

Before you send.

How should I prepare my stems?

Export each track or group as a WAV file, all starting at the same point, with no processing on the master bus. Include a rough mix bounce and a reference track you love.

What's the difference between mixing and mastering?

Mixing balances and shapes all the individual elements of your track. Mastering is the final step that polishes the stereo mix so it sounds consistent and competitive everywhere it's played.

Do you only work with organic house and downtempo?

Those are my specialties, along with world and electronic music, but I'm open to other genres — send it over and I'll tell you honestly if I'm the right fit.

Is the assessment really free?

Yes. Send me a link and I'll reply with what I hear and what the track needs — no obligation.
