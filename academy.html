FAQ

Before you enroll.

Which course should I start with?

If you've never used Ableton, start with the introduction. If you can make loops but struggle to finish tracks, go for Music Production. If your tracks are finished but don't sound professional, take Mixing & Mastering. Not sure? Book the free intro call.

Do I need Ableton Live?

The courses are taught in Ableton Live. If you use another DAW, mention it in your message and we'll see what works.

How do online sessions work?

We meet on a video call with screen sharing, so you can see my session and I can see yours. Work on your own music between sessions.

Can I work on my own tracks?

Yes — that's the point. Every course is personalised and built around your music.
