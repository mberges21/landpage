Biography

Marcelo Bergés is a Colombian producer, multi-instrumentalist and sound explorer with over 24 years making music — starting with his first band at 16, moving through trip-hop, progressive trance, minimal, folktronica and downtempo, arriving today at Organic House, and still evolving. His work blends Andean flutes, guitars, charangos and native percussion with modern electronic production. Music as ceremony — where rhythm becomes breath and silence holds as much power as sound. Born in Bogotá, raised in Costa Rica, currently based in Spain.
