FAQ

Common questions.

Where do you hold sessions?

I'm based on the Costa Blanca (Alicante) and facilitate across the region — and travel for retreats and festivals throughout Spain and Europe.

What is ecstatic dance?

A free-movement practice with no steps, no talking and no alcohol — just music guiding the group through a journey of release, expression and integration.

Do participants need experience?

No. The session is designed so anyone can arrive as they are and move at their own pace.

What do you need from the venue?

A suitable space for movement and a sound system. I can advise on setup and bring what's needed depending on the format.

Can you build a full retreat program?

Yes — offerings can be combined across several days: opening ceremony, dance journeys, live sets, men's work and closing rituals.
