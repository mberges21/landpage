We created Angels together with Fanny, inspired by the magic and perfection of the now — of the instant, of co-creation.

It speaks of those presences, those energies that feel as if someone is there: helping, supporting, holding, guiding. You can't see them, only feel them — and you know they are there. The angels who walk with us through our processes, keeping everything within the perfection that human existence lets us perceive.
