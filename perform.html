Booking FAQ

Before you ask.

Where can you play?

I'm based in Spain and available across Europe. International bookings are welcome — share the location and dates and we'll work out travel.

What do you need technically?

It depends on the format (live act vs. DJ set). The full technical rider is in the press kit, and I'm happy to adapt to your venue's setup.

How long are your sets?

Sets are tailored to your program — from short sunset sessions to extended journeys. Tell me your time slot and I'll suggest the best format.

Can you combine live instruments with a DJ set?

Yes — hybrid sets that mix DJing with live flutes, guitar or charango are one of my favourite formats.
