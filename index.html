About

24 years of music. One thread: the search for depth.

Marcelo Bergés is a Colombian producer, multi-instrumentalist and sound explorer with over 24 years of making music. From trip-hop to progressive trance, deep minimal, folktronica, downtempo and organic house, his sound has always followed one thread: the search for depth.

Today he blends world music and organic electronic with Andean flutes, guitars, charangos, native percussion and ritual atmospheres — music not as entertainment but as a ceremony of presence.

Born in Bogotá, raised on the coasts of Costa Rica, based in Spain. Performances at Envision Festival and Cosmic Convergence; releases on Buddha-Bar Beach, Resueño, Shango Records, Desert Trax, Pipe & Pochet, Organic House Records and more.
