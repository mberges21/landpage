Max for Live · Ableton Live 12

Tools from
my stage.

Devices I build for my own live sets with flute, synth and Ableton. They are free — if one becomes part of your music, a voluntary contribution keeps new ones coming. Early versions: I keep shaping them on stage, and your feedback helps.

Audio effect · for the master

Repeater

A one-knob beat repeater for fills before a drop. One gesture walks from 1/4 to 1/64, locked to Live's tempo, while crush, drive and rhythmic glitches build up — and the song comes back clean when you let go. Ride it with Push, an expression pedal or by holding a step on its screen.

Free · voluntary contribution
Audio effect · for a live instrument

Harmonizer

Adds a second voice to your instrument a 3rd, 4th, 5th or 6th away — inside the scale you choose in Live 12, like a second player reading the same key. Made for live flute.

Free · voluntary contribution
Utility · any track

Clip Scale

Changes Live's scale from the name of the clip that starts playing — "Am", "D dorian", "Chorus [F]". Every device that follows Live's scale follows each section of your song.

Free · voluntary contribution
MIDI effect · with a footswitch

One Button Looper

Four loopers, one footswitch each: tap to record on the next bar, tap again to loop, hold to clear. It records into Live's own clips, so every take stays in your set.

Free · voluntary contribution

FAQ

Good to know.

Why do I pay after ordering?

Every piece is made just for you. I confirm each order personally and send a secure PayPal link with the exact total including shipping to your country — no account needed, cards accepted.

Where is it printed and shipped from?

Pieces are printed on demand by my production partner in Europe, which keeps delivery fast inside the EU. I'll give you the delivery estimate with your payment link.

What if it arrives damaged or misprinted?

Send me a photo within 30 days and I'll arrange a replacement at no cost.

Which size should I choose?

The fit is unisex. If you are between sizes, ask me in the message field and I will send you the size chart with measurements.
