FAQ

Good to know.

Why do I pay after ordering?

Every piece is made just for you. I confirm each order personally and send a secure PayPal link with the exact total including shipping to your country — no account needed, cards accepted.

Where is it printed and shipped from?

Pieces are printed on demand by my production partner in Europe, which keeps delivery fast inside the EU. I'll give you the delivery estimate with your payment link.

What if it arrives damaged or misprinted?

Send me a photo within 30 days and I'll arrange a replacement at no cost.

Which size should I choose?

The fit is unisex. If you are between sizes, ask me in the message field and I will send you the size chart with measurements.
